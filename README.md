## What Is a Spirit Animal?

A spirit animal is believed to be an animal whose characteristics you embody or that serves as a guide in your life. Different cultures have various interpretations:

- **Native American traditions** - Spirit animals (or "power animals") serve as protectors and guides
- **Celtic traditions** - Animal guides connect to nature and the spirit world
- **Modern spirituality** - Spirit animals represent personality traits and inner wisdom

## How to Use These Quizzes

1. **Clear your mind** - Approach the quiz with openness and honesty
2. **Answer intuitively** - Don't overthink; go with your first instinct
3. **Reflect on results** - Consider how the animal's traits relate to your life
4. **Explore further** - Research your spirit animal's deeper symbolism
